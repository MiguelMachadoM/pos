Open the POS, complete an order and print or display the receipt. The header
shows the shop identity configured for that POS. Other shops keep the company
header until you enable the setting there too.

Address is compacted (street, ZIP, city, province). Phone and email print on
one line with a small phone / envelope icon. Long lines wrap. The company VAT
is printed with its label.

Spanish POS (``l10n_es_pos_oca``): also install
``l10n_es_pos_config_shop_identity`` (OCA/l10n-spain) to print the simplified
invoice title and number on one line.
